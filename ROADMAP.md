# Roadmap

PolyTailor is under active development. Planned next:

- Fill that follows the curve of the surrounding surface, with an amount you can set
- Fill offers a surface-fitted choice whenever the outline lies on another model, in either 3D or Topo mode
- Fill directly on curved retopology surfaces, beyond holes that cross a crease
- Faster Relax and Tweak when several large source models are visible
- Better performance on large and dense meshes
- More control over Fill: pin or unpin corners by hand, and relax after filling
- Testing on Linux and macOS

Ideas and bug reports are welcome in [Issues](../../issues).
